# The Quality Intelligence Manifesto

> **Status: v0.9, open draft.** Read it, challenge it, help us write v1.0.
> Target date for v1.0: **November 2026**. Everyone whose contribution is accepted becomes a co-author.

AI is increasing our capacity to produce software much faster than our capacity to understand it.
**Quality Intelligence is how we close that gap.**

---

## What is Quality Intelligence?

**Quality Intelligence (QI)** is the practice and framework of connecting **systems**, **context** and **human judgment** to understand the risk of every change, and to decide with confidence where to invest effort.

*Testing tells us what happened. Quality Intelligence tells us what it means and what to do.*

QI connects three sources of understanding:

| | What it tells us | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Systems** | What the software is actually doing | Code, APIs, logs, traces, environments |
| **Context** | Why it matters | Business rules, requirements, history, incidents, edge cases |
| **People** | What is acceptable | Priorities, judgment, accountability, risk, ethics |

It is not another stage of the SDLC. It is a layer that runs through all of it, from planning to production, and it keeps learning from what production teaches us.

AI can bring the intelligence. Only people can bring the judgment. Trust needs both.

---

## The Manifesto (open draft v0.9)

**We value:**

- **Understanding the risk of each change** over expanding coverage
- **Deciding with evidence** over trusting green indicators
- **Sharing context with people and agents** over concentrating knowledge in a few
- **Learning from causes** over treating symptoms
- **Using AI to understand** over using AI to produce more
- **Being accountable for outcomes** over being accountable for activity

## How to contribute

You don't need to be a GitHub expert, or even have an account.

1. **No GitHub account?** Leave your input here: https://qualityintelligencemanifesto.org/. 
2. **Have a GitHub account?** Open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) to propose or challenge a line, or join the conversation in [Discussions](../../discussions).
3. **Want to edit the text directly?** Open a pull request on `MANIFESTO.md`.

You can:

- **Fill in the blank row:** what do *you* value over what?
- **Challenge a row:** is something wrong, missing or badly worded?

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.
