# Code of Conduct

This manifesto is about building trust. We expect the same from the way we build it together.

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, role, company, language or identity.

## Expected behavior

- **Challenge ideas, not people.** Disagreement is welcome; it's how v1.0 gets better.
- **Assume good intent** and ask before concluding.
- **Be generous with newcomers.** Many contributors may be opening their first issue ever.
- **Ground your arguments** in experience and evidence.
- **Respect the language** people choose to write in (English or Spanish).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Promoting products, services or vendors instead of ideas.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers at sofia@abstracta.us. Reports will be handled confidentially.
