# The Quality Intelligence Manifesto

**Version 0.9, open draft** · [Español](MANIFESTO.es.md)

---

AI is increasing our capacity to produce software much faster than our capacity to understand it.

The bottleneck is no longer producing. It is understanding.

We are uncovering better ways of building trust in software by doing it and by helping others do it. Through this work we have come to value:

**Understanding the risk of each change** over expanding coverage

**Deciding with evidence** over trusting green indicators

**Sharing context with people and agents** over concentrating knowledge in a few

**Learning from causes** over treating symptoms

**Using AI to understand** over using AI to produce more

**Being accountable for outcomes** over being accountable for activity


---

### What we mean by Quality Intelligence

Quality Intelligence is the practice and framework of connecting **systems**, **context** and **human judgment** to understand the risk of every change, and to decide with confidence where to invest effort.

The central question of quality in the age of AI:

> *"What do we need to understand in order to trust this software?"*
