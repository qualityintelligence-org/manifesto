# Contributing to the Quality Intelligence Manifesto

Thank you for helping shape v1.0. Every contribution counts, from a single sentence to a full rewrite of a row.

## What you can contribute

- **Fill in the blank row:** "We value ___ over ___."
- **Challenge a row:** tell us what is wrong, missing, unclear or badly worded, and why.
- **Propose a new row**, or propose removing one.
- **Improve the wording** of the English or Spanish text.
- **Share a real story:** a moment when understanding (or not understanding) risk changed a release decision.

## Three ways to participate

### 1. No GitHub account (easiest)

Use this form: https://qualityintelligencemanifesto.org/
Leave your name the way you want it to appear if your contribution is accepted.

### 2. Ideas and debate → Issues and Discussions

- **[Discussions](../../discussions):** open conversation, questions, early ideas.
- **[Issues](../../issues/new/choose):** a concrete proposal. Pick a template:
  - *Propose a new row*
  - *Challenge or reword a row*

One issue per idea, please. It keeps the debate focused.

### 3. Text changes → Pull requests

1. Fork the repository.
2. Edit `MANIFESTO.md` (and `MANIFESTO.es.md` if you can).
3. Open a pull request explaining **what** you changed and **why**.
4. Link the related issue, if there is one.

Small, focused pull requests are easier to review and discuss.

## Guidelines for a good row

Every row follows the pattern **"[what we value more] over [what still has value]"**.

- **Both sides should be valuable.** The right side is not "bad"; it is just not enough on its own.
- **Short and concrete.** If it doesn't fit on a slide, it's probably too long.
- **About decisions and trust**, not about specific tools or vendors.
- **Grounded in experience.** Real examples make a proposal stronger.

## How decisions are made

- The maintainers review contributions and keep the conversation moving.
- Changes are discussed openly in issues and pull requests before being merged.
- We look for rough consensus, not unanimity. When there is disagreement, maintainers make the final call and explain why.
- Accepted changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Becoming a co-author

If your contribution is accepted, through the form, an issue, a discussion or a pull request, you will be added to [CO-AUTHORS.md](CO-AUTHORS.md) and credited in the v1.0 release.

To be credited, make sure we know the name you want to appear (and optionally your company, role or link).

## Language

Contributions are welcome in **English or Spanish**. We will take care of the translation.

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful, generous and curious. Challenge ideas, not people. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
