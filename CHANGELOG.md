# Changelog

All notable changes to the Quality Intelligence Manifesto are recorded here.

## [Unreleased]

Community contributions collected after the keynote, on the way to v1.0.

## [0.9] - October 13, 2026

First public draft, presented at the Quality Sense Conf 2026 keynote *"From QA to Quality Intelligence"*.

### Added

- Six value statements, plus one blank row for the community to write.
- Definition of Quality Intelligence: connecting systems, context and human judgment.
- English and Spanish versions.
- Contribution guidelines, Code of Conduct and co-authorship policy.
